About Me

I'm Kyle, a 3rd-year BSIT student at PUP Calauan actively seeking a cloud engineering internship. I'm passionate about building resilient, automated, and cost-optimized infrastructure. I come from a full-stack background — writing APIs in Spring Boot, UIs in React, and wiring everything with databases — and now I love wrapping it all in Docker, describing it in Terraform, and shipping it through GitHub Actions.

I've used my coursework and side projects to get hands-on with the exact stack used in junior cloud roles: provisioning EC2s, S3 buckets and security groups on AWS, hardening Dockerfiles with multi-stage builds and non-root users, and running vulnerability scans with Trivy in CI.

When I'm not writing HCL or YAML, I'm troubleshooting a star-topology LAN in the school lab, contributing to group React prototypes, or exploring how to squeeze more performance out of a Spring Boot JAR on a tiny cloud VM. I communicate fluently in both English and Filipino.
